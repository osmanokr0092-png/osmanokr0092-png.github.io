SkyGuard: AI drone anomaly detection and flight risk ranking

Python, scikit-learn, PyTorch, SHAP, Flutter, Firebase. Master’s senior project.

Drone flight logs hold the early signs of most failures, but nobody reads them until after a crash. SkyGuard reads them automatically, decides how dangerous each problem is, explains why, and shows it to the operator on a phone or in a browser.

  • Turned 405 real PX4 flight logs (UAV-SEAD dataset) into 33 behavioural features in 2-second windows.
  • Compared Isolation Forest, Random Forest and an LSTM on 61 unseen test flights; the Random Forest won.
  • Designed a 0–100 risk score and SHAP explanations for every alert.
  • Built the Flutter app for Android and the web: live flight replay with real-time alerts, voice commands in English and Arabic, light and dark themes.
